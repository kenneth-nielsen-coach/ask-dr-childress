These are essays of note from my blog.
 The Legal Argument Package: Forensic or Clinical Psychology (Childress, 2019)
(https://drcraigchildressblog.com/2019/02/10/the-legal-argument-package-forensic-or-clinical-

This essay describes the choice in legal arguments offered to the court.
 Diagnosis of AB-PA (Childress, 2014) (https://drcraigchildressblog.com/2014/06/18/diagnosis/)

 Understanding the Child’s Experience (https://drcraigchildressblog.com/2015/03/06/understanding-

 Complex Trauma: Traumatic Grief (Childress, 2017)
(https://drcraigchildressblog.com/2017/03/31/complex-trauma-traumatic-grief/)
This is an essay about the core of the pathology, the inability of the allied parent to process sadness,
grief, and loss.
 3.04a Harm to the Client (Childress, 2019) (https://drcraigchildressblog.com/2019/04/29/3-04a-
harm-to-the-client/)
This is an essay discussion the violation of Standard 3.04 of the APA ethics code, Avoiding Harm. 
Psychologists are not allowed to hurt anyone.  Anyone.  That includes targeted parents.
Limiting or restricting a parent’s time and involvement with their child to anything less that the most
time and involvement possible harms that parent, and harms the child.  That is in violation of Standard
3.04 Avoiding Harm.  Psychologists are not allowed to hurt people.  Targeted parents are people.  They
count.  Psychologists aer not allowed to do anything to harm the targeted parent – or allied parent – or
child.
A treatment focused orientation rather than a custody orientation can meet Standard 3.04a
 “Psychological Fingerprints” (Childress, 2014) (https://drcraigchildressblog.com/2014/06/30/legal-
psychological-fingerprints/)
This is an essay discussing the narcissistic symptoms displayed by the child, the “psychological
fingerprint” evidence of the child’s control by a narcissistic parent.
 Gardner’s Model of PAS and the Need for a Paradigm Shift (Childress, 2014)
(https://drcraigchildressblog.com/2014/06/18/gardners-model-of-pas/)
This is the second essay posted to my blog.  It describers the need for a paradigm shift away from
Gardner’s model of PAS back to the application of established knowledge of professional psychology.

 Assessing the Behavior-Chain in Parent-Child Conflict (Childress, 2016)
(https://drcraigchildressblog.com/2016/10/18/assessing-the-behavior-chain-in-parent-child-conflict/)
