Patterns of Pathology
There are two primary patterns evidenced by the allied parent. These
patterns tend to have a gender association, although not always.
Narcissistic Dad & Normal-Range Mom
One common pattern-type is a narcissistic father (Dark Triad & Dark
Tetrad) and a normal-range mother. This is the “bad mommy” pattern
and tends to have a higher spousal abuse orientation to it.
One common pattern-type is a borderline mother (Vulnerable Dark
Triad) and a normal-range father. This is the “dangerous man” pattern
and tends to have higher anxiety and false allegations associated with
it.
The associated patterns of pathology are not strictly gender-based,
but the gender of the allied parent is a typically associated feature,
with pathological fathers tending to be Dark Triad / Dark Tetrad
spectrum (narcissistic), and pathological mothers tending to be
Vulnerable Dark Triad (borderline) spectrum.
