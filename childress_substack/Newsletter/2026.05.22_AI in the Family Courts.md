Clarity Amidst Conflict

I am currently incorporating AI into my second opinion review of documents in the family courts. It’s excellent.
AI is going to revolutionize the family courts - AI can provide a high-quality review of many types of documents - mental health reports - court declarations - emails - professional reports - transcripts of depositions - and AI can provide a probability estimate for Path A - Path B causality.

Path A: the targeted parent is abusing the child in some way, thereby creating the child’s authentic attachment pathology toward that parent.
Path B: the allied parent is psychologically abusing the child by creating a shared (induced) persecutory delusion and false (factitious) attachment pathology in the child to meet the allied parent’s own emotional and psychological needs.
AI Review Protocols
I have AI Forensic Protocols to review all kinds of source material - forensic custody evaluations - mental heath treatment reports - mental health treatment notes - court testimony and depositions - professional vitae for 2.01 Competence - and other relevant documentation.
AI brings clarity to confusion. 
In mental health reports, the AI examines for two things, 1) a clinical Path A - Path B analysis based on the symptoms reported, and 2) ethical violations by the mental health professional, possibly in a cascading set of violations: 

Violation to Standard 2.01 - they don’t know the necessary professional knowledge - 2.01 Boundaries of Competence.
Violation to Standard 2.04 - because they don’t know the necessary  knowledge (2.01 violation), they don’t apply the necessary knowledge as the bases for their professional judgments (2.04 violation) - 2.04 Bases for Scientific and Professional Judgments.
Violation to Standard 9.01 - because they don’t know (2.01 violation) or apply (2.04 violation) the established professional knowledge of the disciple, their opinions contained in their recommendations, reports, diagnostic and evaluative statements, including their forensic testimony, are NOT based on information and techniques sufficient to substantiate their findings (9.01 violation) - 9.01 Bases for Assessment.
Violation to Standard 3.04: because they don’t know or apply the necessary knowledge (2.01/2.04 violation), they misdiagnose the pathology in the family (9.01 violation), causing substantial harm to the child and targeted parent (3.04 violation) - 3.04 Avoiding Harm.
The AI looks for that cascade of ethical violations: 2.01 ⟶ 2.04 ⟶ 9.01 ⟶ 3.04.
