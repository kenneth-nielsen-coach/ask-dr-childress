As part of my second-opinion review of forensic custody evaluations, I review the curriculum vita (CV; resume) of the forensic custody evaluator for Standard 2.01 competence in the following relevant domains:

1) Attachment Pathology: demonstrated education, training, and experience in the diagnostic assessment of attachment pathology;

2) Delusional Thought Disorders: demonstrated education, training, and experience in the diagnostic assessment of delusional thought disorders;

3) Custody Evaluations: level of professional involvement in conducting forensic custody evaluations.
